Privacy Policy — Finance Flash Uploader

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Finance Flash Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool operated by the owner of the Finance Flash channel. It is used exclusively to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Data the App accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts, granted by the owner.
  • Basic channel/account information needed to confirm the upload destination.

How data is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status on the owner's own accounts. The App does not read, collect, analyze or store data about viewers or any other users.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No data is used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok

Access to TikTok can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Apps and services permissions.

Data deletion

Revoking access as described above stops all use; locally stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.

Contact

Questions: contact the Finance Flash channel via its YouTube "About" page.
